## 🇬🇧 English Version

### 📋 Overview

BusinessRadar is a web-based platform designed specifically for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) including food services and individual businesses. The platform intelligently displays upcoming public events within customizable search ranges on an interactive map, enabling business managers to easily identify and capitalize on commercial opportunities for future planning.

**🎯 Demo Presentation:** [View on Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1TkGB4P0EvWyFs2KsBtTQNaFSEUQ3saorDcyjx8ihf9U/edit?usp=sharing)

### 🎯 Core Objectives

BusinessRadar empowers MSMEs to:

1. **📦 Inventory Management** - Arrange stocks strategically to handle potential demand increases during events
2. **👥 Staff Optimization** - Adjust staff schedules to ensure adequate personnel on event days, preventing customer loss due to wait times
3. **📢 Marketing Opportunities** - Promote and advertise businesses to large audiences, including potential customers attending events

### 💼 Real-World Use Cases

#### 🍽️ Restaurant Scenario
A local restaurant wants to stay informed about events happening within the upcoming week and within a 1-kilometer radius. BusinessRadar provides daily updates about events for the rest of the current week and the following week, prompting the restaurant to:
- Check and adjust inventory levels
- Optimize staff schedules for anticipated crowds
- Increase promotional activities on event days

#### 🎨 Artist Example
An artist selling handmade pieces seeks updates about events, particularly art-related gatherings and market days. Using BusinessRadar, she configures notifications for relevant events within a 10km radius with 1-month advance notice, enabling her to:
- Contact event organizers proactively
- Prepare appropriate artwork for specific events
- Plan her sales calendar effectively

### 🚀 Getting Started

5. **Plan Ahead** - Use event information for inventory, staffing, and marketing decisions

## 🇨🇳 中文版本

### 📋 项目概述

BusinessRadar是专为中小微企业（包括餐饮服务和个体经营者）设计的智能商机发现平台。该平台可在交互式地图上显示自定义搜索范围内的即将举行的公共活动，并进行标注，极大地方便企业管理者进行未来规划和商机把握。

**🎯 功能演示:** [查看演示文稿](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1TkGB4P0EvWyFs2KsBtTQNaFSEUQ3saorDcyjx8ihf9U/edit?usp=sharing)

### 🎯 核心目标

BusinessRadar帮助中小微企业：

1. **📦 库存管理** - 战略性安排库存，应对活动期间的潜在需求增长
2. **👥 人员优化** - 调整员工排班，确保活动日有足够人手，避免因等待时间过长而流失客户
3. **📢 营销机会** - 向包括潜在客户在内的大量受众推广和宣传业务

### 💼 实际应用场景

#### 🍽️ 餐厅应用场景
某本地餐厅希望了解未来一周内1公里范围内的活动信息。BusinessRadar每日提供本周剩余时间和下周的活动更新，提醒餐厅：
- 检查并调整库存水平
- 针对预期客流优化员工排班
- 在活动日增加促销活动

#### 🎨 艺术家应用实例
一位销售手工艺品的艺术家寻求活动更新信息，特别是艺术相关聚会和市集日。通过BusinessRadar，她设置10公里范围内提前1个月的相关活动通知，使她能够：
- 主动联系活动组织者
- 为特定活动准备合适的艺术作品
- 有效规划销售日程
