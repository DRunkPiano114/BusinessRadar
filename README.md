## 🇬🇧 English Version

### Overview
This project is a website for micro-small-medium enterprises (MSMEs), such as food services and individual businesses, showing upcoming events within the region or holidays that could lead to business opportunities. 

The aim is to help MSMEs: 
  1. Arrange their stocks to make sure they have enough to handle the potential increase in demand, 
  2. Adjust staff schedules to ensure enough staff on the event day to promptly serve customers and prevent losing them due to excessive wait times
  3. promote and advertise their business to a large number of people, which could include their potential customers



** Demo:** [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1TkGB4P0EvWyFs2KsBtTQNaFSEUQ3saorDcyjx8ihf9U/edit?usp=sharing)

### Examples:
A restaurant wants to be updated about events in the upcoming week and within 1 kilometer. Events would be updated every day about upcoming events the rest of the week and the following week prompting it to check stocks, adjust staff schedules, and increase promotions on the day of the event. 

An artist selling their art pieces wanted to be updated about events, especially art-related events and market days. She chose to be updated about related events within a 10km radius and 1 month in advance, allowing them to reach out to organizers and prepare their pieces.

## 🇨🇳 中文版本

### 概述
本项目是一个面向中小微企业（如餐饮服务和个体经营者）的网站，展示区域内即将举办的活动或可能带来商机的节假日。

项目旨在协助中小微企业：
  1. 合理安排库存，确保能满足潜在需求增长
  2. 调整员工排班，保障活动当日人员充足，及时服务顾客，避免因过长等待时间流失客源
  3. 向大量人群推广宣传业务，覆盖潜在客户群体
